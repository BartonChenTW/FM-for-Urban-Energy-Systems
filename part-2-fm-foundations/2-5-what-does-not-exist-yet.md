# 2.5 What Does Not Exist Yet
{: .no_toc }

{% include page-status.html %}

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

- No foundation model for **multi-carrier urban energy system operation**.
- No foundation model for **energy system design/planning models**.
- No **benchmark** for either.
- No agreed **representation or interchange format** that preserves assumptions alongside structure.
- No **agreed basic element for buildings**, and no published treatment of the choice as a research question rather than an implementation detail.
- No foundation model representing a **decision space** — the set of possible interventions on a system — alongside its state ([G9](../part-6-outlook/6-1-open-gaps.html#g9)).

This is the gap the rest of this book is written into: [Part 4](../part-4-directions/index.html) surveys plausible directions broadly, and [Part 5](../part-5-case-study/index.html) works through one specific proposal — a foundation model for multi-carrier energy hubs — in depth.
